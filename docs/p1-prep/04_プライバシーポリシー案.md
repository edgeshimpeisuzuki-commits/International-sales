## Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{date}}

{{agent_name}} ("we", "us") provides overseas sales representation for Japanese manufacturers, including OGINO SHOTEN Co., Ltd. ("the Manufacturer"). This policy explains how we handle personal information of people we contact for business purposes.

### 1. Who we are
{{agent_name}}, {{agent_address}}, Japan. Contact: {{privacy_contact_email}}

### 2. What we collect
- Business contact details that your organization has made public, such as company name, business email address, and job title.
- The content of emails you send to us, and records of our communication (dates, replies, meeting notes).
We do not buy contact lists, and we do not collect information from sites that prohibit it.

### 3. How we use it
- To introduce the Manufacturer's products to your organization and respond to your inquiries.
- To arrange meetings, prepare proposals and quotations, and follow up on business discussions.
- To keep records of opt-out requests so that we do not contact you again.

### 4. Sharing with the Manufacturer
We contact you on behalf of the Manufacturer, using an email address provided by the Manufacturer. Emails you send are received in the Manufacturer's mailbox and can be read by both the Manufacturer and us. We share your business contact details and our communication with the Manufacturer only for the purposes above.

### 5. Use of service providers outside Japan
We use cloud services, including AI services, to draft emails, organize replies, and store records. These providers include Anthropic, PBC and Google LLC, located in the United States. {{US_regime_description}} We choose providers that offer contractual data protection commitments, and we turn off settings that allow our data to be used for training their AI models.

### 6. How long we keep it
We keep business communication records for up to {{retention_years}} years after our last contact. After you opt out, we keep only your email address and the opt-out date so that we never contact you again.

### 7. Your choices and rights
- Opt out: reply "unsubscribe" to any email or use {{unsubscribe_url}}. We apply your request within 24 hours.
- Access, correction, or deletion: email {{privacy_contact_email}}. We will respond without undue delay. Records kept in the Manufacturer's mailbox will be handled by the Manufacturer at our request.

### 8. Security
Access to our records is limited to authorized members, and all accounts use two-step verification.

### 9. Changes
We may update this policy. The latest version is always posted on this page.
